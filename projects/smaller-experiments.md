## How to add a future code exhibit

Choose a small, non-sensitive excerpt whose authorship is established. Explain the problem, the key decision, and one tradeoff. Identify the original source and approximate date. A short explanation should help the reader understand the work without needing to execute an obsolete application.

No screenshot is presented as historical evidence unless it is an actual preserved image with relevant context. A new diagram or recreation should be labeled with its new creation date.
